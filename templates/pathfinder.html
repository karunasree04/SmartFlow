📐 How Dijkstra Works Here

The road network has 8 intersections (nodes) and 11 roads (edges) with distance weights. Dijkstra starts at your selected source, maintains a min-heap of (distance, node) pairs, and greedily visits the closest unvisited node at every step — relaxing all its edges. This guarantees the globally shortest path is found when the destination is reached.

The Smart Route adds a congestion penalty to certain roads based on your latest vehicle count analysis — so the algorithm naturally avoids those roads, finding a longer but faster real-world path. This is exactly how Google Maps, Apple Maps, and Waze work in real life.

⏱ Time: O((V+E) log V) 💾 Space: O(V) V = 8 nodes   E = 11 edges
